The Leads

Tanaz

THE BRIDE

Tanaz reads a lot — like, "keeps a spreadsheet of every book she's read, how long it took, and her immediate thoughts" a lot. A proud marathon survivor, she also loves running, and one of her favorite parts of the last few years has been watching Vince take up the sport. Sharing something she loves with him has been incredibly special, and their runs together are some of the best parts of her week.

When she's not reading or running, she's usually in the kitchen testing a new recipe or cooking with friends. If she invites you to join, whatever you do, don't ask her to deviate from the recipe. She will not. She cannot.

In short, Tanaz is happiest with a book in hand, running shoes nearby, and a kitchen full of people she loves (who are following the recipe exactly).

Vince

THE GROOM

A creative soul with a passion for music and technology. Former band member turned tech enthusiast, always ready with a joke and a smile.

Whether he's coding, making music, or planning the perfect surprise, Vince brings joy and laughter to everything he does.
