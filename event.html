FAQs

When is the RSVP deadline?

Please RSVP by January 31, 2025. And please book your flights and hotel rooms before you confirm attendance. Information about both can be found on the Travel page of this website.

What should I wear?

Each event has a specific dress code listed. Thursday is Indian or western cocktail attire, Friday is colorful Indian attire, and Saturday is Indian or western formal. If you need help figuring out where to buy an Indian outfit, let me know!

Are children welcome?

Yes, but please let us know if you'll be bringing your kid(s), so we can account for them.

Can I bring a plus one?

If you've been allocated a plus one, their name will appear on your invitation. If you have questions, please reach out to us directly.

Where can I share my dietary restrictions?

Please note any dietary restrictions on the RSVP form.

Is there parking available?

There are plenty of parking garages in NYC. But, we highly recommend using rideshare services or the subway to avoid the expense and hassle of parking in the city.
