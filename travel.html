Visiting New York City

Getting There

✈️ By Air

Fly into JFK, Newark (EWR), or LaGuardia (LGA). JFK and Newark offer the easiest access to Manhattan via AirTrain and subway/train connections.

🚊 By Train

Penn Station and Grand Central Terminal are the major rail hubs, with Amtrak and regional trains.

🚗 By Car

Parking in NYC is expensive ($40-60/day). We recommend using public transportation once in the city.

Where to Stay

Room blocks available at:

  • 🏨 The Plaza Hotel (Wedding Venue) - Use code TANVINCE2025
  • 🏨 The Jane Hotel - Budget-friendly option in West Village
  • 🏨 1 Hotel Brooklyn Bridge - Eco-luxury with skyline views

Neighborhoods to consider: Midtown (near venues), Brooklyn (hipster vibes), or Lower Manhattan (great restaurants).

NYC Tips

🗺️ Getting Around

Download Citymapper for subway navigation. Get a MetroCard at any subway station ($2.90 per ride).

🍽️ Dining

Tipping: 18-20% at restaurants, $1-2 per drink at bars. Try Joe's Pizza, Russ & Daughters, and Din Tai Fung.

☀️ Weather

May weather: 60-75°F (15-24°C), pack layers!

🎭 Must-See

Central Park, Brooklyn Bridge, The Met, Broadway shows, and Top of the Rock for skyline views.

Eat & Drink

  • 🥯 Russ & Daughters - Classic NYC bagels
  • 🍕 Joe's Pizza - Iconic NY slice
  • 🍜 Ippudo - Amazing ramen
  • 🥟 Din Tai Fung - Soup dumplings

Things to Do

  • 🗽 Statue of Liberty - Book tickets in advance
  • 🌳 Central Park - Perfect for a morning stroll
  • 🎭 Broadway Show - Book tickets on Broadway.com
  • 🏛️ The Met - World-class art museum
  • 🌉 Brooklyn Bridge - Walk or bike across
  • 🏙️ Top of the Rock - Best skyline views
  • 🛍️ SoHo - Shopping and galleries
